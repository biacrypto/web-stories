What Is a Crypto ETF?

A share you buy on a normal stock exchange that tracks Bitcoin’s price. No wallet, no seed phrase, no blockchain — just your brokerage app.

ETF vs Real Crypto

ETF: dead simple, no staking, 0.2–1% yearly fees, market-hours only. Real crypto: full control, staking rewards, but YOU guard the keys.
