A Wallet Holds Keys, Not Coins

Your bitcoin lives on the blockchain. The wallet holds your keys — the proof you own it. Lose the keys, lose the coins. No password reset exists.

BIA CRYPTO

Pick Your Wallet Type

Mobile wallet (free, best to start) · Hardware wallet ($50–200+, best security) · Exchange account (easy, but they hold your keys — not you).

BIA CRYPTO

Download From the Official Site Only

Never install a wallet from an ad or a YouTube link. Type the official site yourself. Fake wallet apps are one of crypto’s most common scams.

BIA CRYPTO

The Seed Phrase — Your Master Key

12–24 words = total control of your bitcoin. Write them on PAPER (two copies, two places). Never photograph them. Test the restore once.

BIA CRYPTO

Your First Receive Address

Tap “Receive” to get your address + QR code. Send a tiny test amount first ($10), wait for it to land, then send the rest.

BIA CRYPTO

Lock It Down

Strong app PIN · biometric lock · never share your seed phrase with ANYONE. No support agent will ever need it — anyone who asks is a scammer.
