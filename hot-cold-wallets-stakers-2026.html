Hot vs Cold Wallets:
Which Is Safer
for Stakers?

The beginner security guide to where your staked crypto should actually live.

BIA CRYPTO

Hot = Keys Online

Phone apps, browser extensions, exchange wallets. Free, fast, two minutes to set up — and always one bad click from trouble.

BIA CRYPTO

Cold = Keys Offline

Hardware devices like Ledger or Trezor. Keys never touch the internet. Remote theft becomes extraordinarily hard.

BIA CRYPTO

Yes, You Can Stake Cold

Connect the hardware wallet to Phantom, Ledger Live, Keplr or similar — the device signs each staking transaction with a physical button press.

BIA CRYPTO

The Split I Actually Use

Hot wallet = small operating cash for gas and moves. Cold wallet = the vault holding long-term staked positions. 5-15% hot is my rule of thumb.

BIA CRYPTO

What a Cold Wallet Can't Fix

Phished seed phrases, tampered devices from resellers, slashing, lock-ups, price crashes. The wallet protects keys — not your position.
