Is a Crypto Swap
Taxable in
the US?

Yes — the IRS treats a swap as a sale, even if you never touch a dollar. Here is the rule, with numbers.

BIA CRYPTO

Short Answer: Yes

Crypto is property, not money, in IRS eyes. Trading Bitcoin for Ether counts as selling the Bitcoin — gain or loss on the spot.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example

Example: buy 1 ETH for $2,000, swap it when it is worth $3,500. Gain = $1,500. Your new coins take a $3,500 cost basis.

BIA CRYPTO

Short vs Long Term

Held one year or less: taxed as ordinary income, 10% to 37%. Held more than a year: 0%, 15% or 20%. Every swap restarts the clock.

BIA CRYPTO

The Wallet Trap

Since 2025, cost basis is tracked wallet by wallet. You cannot borrow a cheaper basis from another exchange to shrink a gain.

BIA CRYPTO

The 1099-DA Trap

First 1099-DA forms show gross proceeds, not your profit. Copy proceeds as gain and you overpay. Your records supply the missing half.

BIA CRYPTO

Small Swaps Count Too

USDC for USDT is still a disposal — usually a tiny gain. Gas fees paid in crypto are disposals as well. Record everything.
