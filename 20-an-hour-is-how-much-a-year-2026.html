The Short Answer

$20 x 40 hours x 52 weeks = $41,600 a year. That is $160 a day for an 8-hour shift, before taxes.

The Formula

A standard full-time year is 2,080 hours — 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. Annual salary = wage x hours per week x weeks per year.
