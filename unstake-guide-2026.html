Unstaking Has a Queue.

Every proof-of-stake network makes you WAIT before your coins come back. This is called unbonding.

ETH: 14–21 Days

The validator exit queue is the slowest of the majors — ~850,000 ETH was waiting in Oct 2026. No rewards while you wait.

SOL: 2–3 Days

One full epoch cooldown in your wallet. Hit Deactivate, then Withdraw after the timer ends.

DOT: 28 Days — The Longest

A full month with zero rewards and zero access. Never stake DOT you might need soon.

ADA: No Lock At All

Cardano never locks your coins — spend staked ADA anytime and keep earning until you move it.

ATOM: 21 Days

Undelegate in Keplr, wait 3 weeks, funds appear automatically. Plan around events, not prices.
