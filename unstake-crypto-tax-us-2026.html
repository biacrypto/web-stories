Do You Pay Tax
When You
Unstake Crypto?

The US answer, in plain English — unstaking is not the same as selling.

Short Answer: Usually No

Same coins back to your wallet is not a sale. No disposal, no capital gains tax on the unstake itself.

BIA CRYPTO

The Rule: No Disposal, No Tax

Crypto is property. Tax hits disposals and income — not moving your own coins from staked to unstaked.

BIA CRYPTO

Trap 1: Locked Rewards

Rewards locked until unstaking become income when you can finally use them — Rev. Rul. 2023-14, dominion and control.

BIA CRYPTO

Trap 2: Liquid Staking

Swapping stETH back to ETH may count as a disposal. The IRS has never ruled on it — take one view, consistently.

BIA CRYPTO

Trap 3: Selling Straight After

Unstake: no tax. The sale right after: capital gain or loss against your original basis. Two separate events.

Worked Example

10 ETH bought for $20,000, unstaked intact: no tax on return. Sold later for $30,000: gain $10,000. Holding period never reset.
