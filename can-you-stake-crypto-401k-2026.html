Can You Stake
Crypto in a 401k?

No in a normal employer plan — yes only in a self-directed Solo 401k.

BIA CRYPTO

The Short Answer

Your employer’s 401k offers funds, not coins. You never own the coin, so you cannot stake it. Even a crypto ETF does not stake.

BIA CRYPTO

What Changed in 2025

In May 2025 the Labor Department dropped its “extreme care” crypto warning. An August 2025 executive order pushed the door further open.

BIA CRYPTO

The Only Route

A Solo 401k for the self-employed can hold crypto directly — and crypto held directly can be staked through a third-party provider.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example

$50,000 of Ether staked at an illustrative 4% earns about $2,000 a year. Outside a plan, a 22% bracket owes about $440 tax that year. Inside a traditional Solo 401k: $0 that year.

BIA CRYPTO

Roll Over an Old 401k

An old employer 401k can roll directly into a Solo 401k — plan to plan, no tax withheld. Never take the check yourself: 20% withholding and a 60-day deadline.

BIA CRYPTO

The Trap That Kills the Account

Running your own validator or moving your own coins in is likely a prohibited transaction. The penalty is not a fine — the whole account is taxed as distributed.
