You Don't Need a Whole Bitcoin

One bitcoin splits into 100 million "satoshis." You can start with $25 — a tiny fraction is completely normal.

BIA CRYPTO

Route 1: A Regulated Exchange

Coinbase, Kraken, Bitstamp — ID check, tap buy, you own real bitcoin. Fees run 0.5-2%; coins stay in their custody until you move them.

BIA CRYPTO

Route 2: A Spot Bitcoin ETF

Funds like BlackRock's IBIT hold real bitcoin; you buy shares in your normal brokerage. No wallets, no keys — but no real coins either.

BIA CRYPTO

Route 3: Small Automatic Buys

$25 every Friday beats one terrified lump sum. High weeks buy less, low weeks buy more. Two taps to automate.

BIA CRYPTO

What to Avoid

"Double your bitcoin" schemes, 100x leverage, Telegram strangers offering help. This is where beginners lose money — not in bitcoin itself.
