What Is a
Seed Phrase?

12 words = your entire crypto wallet. The master key beginners must protect.

BIA CRYPTO

It Is NOT a Password

A seed phrase RECREATES your whole wallet on any device. Whoever holds the words holds your crypto — the actual thing.

BIA CRYPTO

Rule #1: Paper Only

Write it by hand on paper. Two copies, two locations. Never screenshot it, never store it in notes, email, or the cloud.

BIA CRYPTO

No Legit Support EVER Asks

Real wallets and exchanges will NEVER ask for your seed phrase. Anyone who does is a thief — full stop.

Watch for These Scams

Fake support DMs. Fake wallet apps from ads. "Verify to claim an airdrop." All of them want one thing: your 12 words.

Exposed? Act FAST

Typed it somewhere shady? Make a NEW wallet with a fresh phrase and move everything. Pay the gas fee — speed beats cost here.
