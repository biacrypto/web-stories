Legit, and Watched

Running since 2012, listed on Nasdaq as COIN since 2021, and in the S&P 500 since May 2025. US state licences plus a New York BitLicense and UK FCA registration.

The SEC Case Is Over

The SEC sued in June 2023 over unregistered trading and staking. It dismissed the case in February 2025 — no penalty, no business change. The wider token rules are still unsettled.

Insurance: Read the Split

USD at partner banks: up to $250,000 FDIC pass-through. Crypto and USDC: NO FDIC, NO SIPC. Private crime insurance covers only part of hot-wallet theft — never your own hacked account.

The 2025 Data Breach

Bribed support staff stole data for 69,461 customers. No passwords, keys or funds taken — but names, IDs and balances now fuel fake Coinbase support scams. Hang up on any caller asking you to move crypto.

October 2026: Vendor Leak

Analytics vendor Amplitude exposed contact data for a small subset of customers. Coinbase says its own systems were not breached and funds were not affected — and disputes the 70,000 figure.

Accounts Can Be Restricted

Password changes, new devices and big withdrawals can trigger reviews. Buying may stop while sending crypto out and cashing dollars out still work. Full freezes are rare — court orders or sanctions only.
