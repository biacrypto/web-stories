What "MPC" Means

Multi-Party Computation: your private key is split into pieces called shares. The full key never exists in one place.

BIA CRYPTO

The Usual Setup: 2-of-3

Three shares exist; any two can sign. One share alone is completely useless — even a thief's.

BIA CRYPTO

What MPC Kills

The lost-paper problem. No 24 words to misplace, photograph, or run through the wash. Share rotation replaces panic migration.

BIA CRYPTO

The Catch Nobody Tells You

Most consumer MPC wallets keep one share on the provider's servers. No paper to lose — but now you depend on a company and an internet connection.

BIA CRYPTO

My Blunt Verdict

MPC protects you from yourself; a seed phrase protects you from everyone else. Lose things? Go MPC. Demand independence? Seed phrase + hardware wallet.
