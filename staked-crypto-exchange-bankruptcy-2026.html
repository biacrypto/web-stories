Short Answer: You Become a Creditor

Staked coins on an exchange are usually treated as the exchange's property. You cannot unstake, sell, or move them.

BIA CRYPTO

The Celsius Ruling

In January 2023 a US court ruled Celsius Earn crypto belonged to Celsius — about 600,000 accounts, roughly $4.2 billion.

BIA CRYPTO

No Unstaking in a Freeze

An unstaking request is still a withdrawal. While the platform is frozen, there is no queue to join.

BIA CRYPTO

Your Claim Is Priced in Dollars

Balances become claims fixed at filing-date prices. A later rally does not raise your claim.

BIA CRYPTO

What Past Cases Paid

Celsius first paid about 57-58% of dollar claims. Voyager about 35.7% at first. FTX about 118% of petition-date dollars — not coins.

BIA CRYPTO

Self-Custody Is Different

Stake from your own wallet and an exchange bankruptcy cannot touch the on-chain stake. Slashing and key risks remain.
