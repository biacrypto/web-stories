Are Crypto Staking
Rewards Taxed
in the UK?

HMRC treats them as income first — and CGT second. Here is the two-step rule, with numbers.

BIA CRYPTO

Short Answer: Yes

Staking rewards are usually miscellaneous income in the UK, taxed at 20%, 40% or 45% when you receive them — not when you sell.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example

Example: £1,800 of rewards at 20% = £360 income tax. Sell those tokens later for £2,400 and the £600 gain sits inside the £3,000 annual exempt amount.

Two Taxes, One Set
of Tokens

Step 1: income tax on the sterling value at receipt. Step 2: CGT only on the rise since receipt. Never tax the full sale price twice.

BIA CRYPTO

The Section 104 Pool

Rewards enter your pooled average cost at their receipt value. Example: 10 ETH at £2,000 plus 1 reward ETH at £250 = 11 ETH costing £2,250.

BIA CRYPTO

The £1,000 Allowance

Total trading and miscellaneous income of £1,000 or less may be covered — per person, not per platform. It does not wipe out a later capital gain.

UK vs US: Locked
Rewards Differ

The US waits for control of the rewards. HMRC taxes value at receipt — so locked rewards are a grey area here. Get advice, do not import the US answer.
