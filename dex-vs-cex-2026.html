Two Ways to Trade

CEX: you hand coins to a company (Coinbase, Binance) and trade on their private ledger. DEX: you connect your own wallet and a smart contract swaps your tokens.

BIA CRYPTO

The Vending Machine

A DEX runs on liquidity pools — users stock a smart contract with token pairs, and it reprices itself after every swap. Providers earn ~0.3% per trade.

BIA CRYPTO

Where Your Money Goes

CEX: your deposit becomes an IOU on their servers. DEX: tokens move wallet-to-pool in one atomic on-chain transaction — the exchange never holds them.

BIA CRYPTO

The Real Risks

CEX risk: the company fails or gets hacked (FTX, Bybit 2025). DEX risk: smart-contract bugs, phishing sites, scam tokens — and your own mistakes. No undo.

My Blunt Rule

The CEX is my cash register (fiat in, fiat out). My wallet + a DEX is my safe. Money I can't lose sleep over never sits on an exchange overnight.
