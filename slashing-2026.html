What Is Slashing
in Crypto?

The staking penalty beginners never see coming — explained in 60 seconds.

BIA CRYPTO

The Blockchain Burns Your Coins?

Slashing is an automatic protocol penalty: a validator misbehaves, and the chain permanently destroys part of its staked tokens.

BIA CRYPTO

Trigger #1: Double-Signing

A validator signs two blocks at the same height — usually duplicate keys on two servers. Cosmos: 5% slash + permanent ban.

BIA CRYPTO

Trigger #2: Bad Votes (Ethereum)

Voting for two conflicting checkpoints. ~1 ETH initial penalty — but coordinated attacks can cost everything.

BIA CRYPTO

Does It Hit YOUR Coins?

Cosmos + Polkadot: YES, delegators share the slash. Ethereum pools: mostly shielded. Solana + Cardano: NO protocol slashing at all.

BIA CRYPTO

How Often? Rare.

Most validators are never slashed. Incidents trace to operator error — duplicate keys, bad failover — not random punishment.

BIA CRYPTO

Protect Yourself

Pick reputable validators, diversify on Cosmos chains, prefer insured pools on Ethereum — and remember: price crashes dwarf slashing risk.
