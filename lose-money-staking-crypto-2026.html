Short Answer: Yes

Risk 1: The Price Falls

$10,000 staked at 5% earns about $500 a year. A 30% price fall loses $3,000 — six years of rewards, gone.

Risk 2: Slashing

Only about 0.03% of Ethereum validators have ever been slashed. Cardano has no slashing — and Polkadot nominators have been unslashable since July 2026.

Risk 3: You Cannot Sell

ADA: no wait. SOL: 2–3 days. DOT: about 2 days. ATOM: 21 days. ETH: the exit queue ran to about 3 weeks in late October 2026.

Risk 4: Inflation Eats Yield

Across 20 chains the average is 6.01% headline APY — but only 2.97% real yield once inflation is subtracted. SOL: 6.59% becomes 1.66%.

Risk 5 + The Tax Trap

Exchanges can fail with your coins inside. And in the US, rewards are taxed as income when received — even if the coin then crashes 50%.
