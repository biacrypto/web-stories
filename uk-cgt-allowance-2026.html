Use Your £3,000
CGT Allowance on Crypto

Short answer: UK residents can make £3,000 of crypto gains tax-free this year. Here is how to actually use it.

BIA CRYPTO

What the Allowance Is

£3,000 per person, all assets combined. It covers your gains — not what you sold. Resets every 6 April. No carry forward.

BIA CRYPTO

Gains Count, Not Proceeds

Sell £7,000 of ETH with a £3,000 gain? Zero tax due. The allowance cares about your profit, not the sale price.

BIA CRYPTO

The 30-Day Rebuy Trap

Sell and rebuy the same coin within 30 days and HMRC matches the sale to the repurchase cost. Wait 31+ days — or buy a different coin.

Double It With Your Spouse

Transfers between spouses are CGT-free, and your spouse has their own £3,000 allowance. £6,000 tax-free per household.

BIA CRYPTO

Losses and Costs Count Too

Crystallise losses to offset gains (claim within 4 years). Exchange fees and gas come off the gain as well.

The £12,000 Reporting Trap

Gains under £3,000 but proceeds over £12,000? You must still report to HMRC — even with no tax to pay.
