Gifted Crypto
in the UK:
Who Pays Tax?

The giver can owe tax — even though they got nothing back.

BIA CRYPTO

The Short Answer

The receiver usually pays nothing. The giver is taxed as if they sold the crypto at market value on the day of the gift.

BIA CRYPTO

The Trap

A gift is a disposal for Capital Gains Tax. HMRC treats the giver as receiving the market value — even when they received nothing.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example

2 ETH bought at £1,500 each, gifted at £3,200 each: gain £3,400. After the £3,000 allowance, £400 taxable — £72 at 18%.

BIA CRYPTO

What the Receiver Pays Later

Nothing on receipt. Their cost is the market value on the day of the gift — £6,400 in the example. Tax only when they sell.

BIA CRYPTO

Spouse? No Tax Today

Gifts to a spouse or civil partner are no gain, no loss. But the spouse takes over the giver’s original cost — the bill waits, it does not vanish.

BIA CRYPTO

Gift From Abroad

A UK receiver pays no UK tax on receipt. The giver’s tax depends on their own country’s rules. Keep the date, amount and value.
