APY vs APR
in Crypto

The difference that quietly costs beginners money — explained in 30 seconds.

BIA CRYPTO

Same Number,
Different Meaning

APR = simple yearly rate, no compounding. APY = the rate WITH compounding baked in. Never compare one to the other.

BIA CRYPTO

The Math: $10,000 at 5%

5% APR paid out: $10,500. Same 5% APR compounded daily: $10,513 (5.13% APY). Small gap — but watch what happens at real crypto rates.

BIA CRYPTO

At 20%, The Gap Grows

20% APR compounded daily becomes 22.13% APY — an extra $213 per $10,000. Now the difference between APR and APY actually matters.

The Token Trap

"5% APY" paid in a platform token that drops 30%? Your $10,000 becomes $7,350. The APY was technically correct — your wallet doesn't care.

The 6 Questions I Ask

APR or APY? How often does it compound? What is it paid in? Where does the yield come from? Lock-up catch? Fees and taxes?

BIA CRYPTO

Higher APY Does NOT Mean Better

5% on tokenized Treasury bills and 25% on a brand-new farm are different risk universes. Higher yield is the market telling you it's riskier.
