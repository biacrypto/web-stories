Are Crypto Losses
Tax Deductible
in the UK?

Claim them — or lose them. The rules, the deadline and the traps.

BIA CRYPTO

The Short Answer

Yes — a loss cuts your capital gains tax. It does not cut your income tax, and HMRC sends no refund.

BIA CRYPTO

The Deadline That Matters

You have 4 years from the end of the tax year to claim a loss. 2022/23 losses must be claimed by 5 April 2027.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example

£20,000 gain minus a £12,000 loss minus the £3,000 allowance = £5,000 taxable. At 24%, that is £1,200 — not £4,080.

BIA CRYPTO

The 30-Day Trap

Sell at a loss and buy the same token back within 30 days, and most of the loss disappears. US advice does not apply here.

BIA CRYPTO

What Does NOT Count

A coin down 90% that you still hold. Lost private keys. A "Lost" tag in a tracking app. None of these is a loss yet.

BIA CRYPTO

Negligible Value Claims

If a token is genuinely worthless — dead project, no market — a Section 24 claim can treat it as disposed of. A price fall alone does not qualify.
