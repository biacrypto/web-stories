What Is Crypto
Restaking?

Short Answer: One Stake, Twice Committed

Restaking takes ETH already staked on Ethereum and commits it again to secure other services (AVSs) — for extra rewards and extra risk.

BIA CRYPTO

How It Works

Validator opts in to extra slashing rules. Most people use liquid restaking tokens (LRTs) — ether.fi, Renzo, Kelp DAO, Puffer — instead of restaking directly.

BIA CRYPTO

The Worked Example: $10,000

Plain staking at 4% = $400 a year. Restaking at 6% = $600. The extra $200 doubles the risk surface — more slashing rules, more protocol layers.

The 2026 Reality Check

$10.8B locked across restaking — but only about $30,700 in weekly fees (Sept 2026). Most extra yield is paid in printed tokens, not real fees.

Slashing Is Live Now

EigenLayer slashing went live April 2025. In April 2026, the Kelp DAO exploit drained ~$292M via a bridge — EigenLayer contracts untouched, the LRT layer broke.

Who Should Skip It?
