The Short Answer

$25 x 40 hours x 52 weeks = $52,000 a year. That is $200 a day for an 8-hour shift, before taxes.

The Formula

A standard full-time year is 2,080 hours — 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. Annual salary = wage x hours per week x weeks per year.

Take-Home Pay

For a single filer in 2026, about $43,962 a year lands after federal income tax and FICA — roughly $3,664 a month. State tax is not included.

Overtime Pays 1.5x

The overtime rate is $37.50 an hour. Five overtime hours a week adds $9,750 a year, taking gross pay to $61,750.

Unpaid Time Off Costs You

Two unpaid weeks cuts the year to $50,000. Four unpaid weeks cuts it to $48,000. Paid time off does not change the total.
