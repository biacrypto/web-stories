Reason 1: Gresham’s Law

“Bad money drives out good.” You spend the depreciating dollars and hoard the appreciating Bitcoin. Over 68% of all BTC sits untouched for a year+.

Reason 2: Every Purchase = A Tax Form

The IRS treats crypto as property. Spending Bitcoin = disposing of an asset = capital gains on every coffee. Reported on Form 8949 and Schedule D.

Reason 3: The Regret Machine

Spend $200 of BTC today and your brain replays it as “$400 wasted” when it rallies. Nobody enjoys that feeling — so nobody spends.

Reason 4: Real-World Friction

On-chain fees eat small purchases. Lightning fixes it, but wallets still feel beta. 23,000+ merchants take BTC — but the dollar is tap-and-done.

The Fix: Spend and Replace

Pay in Bitcoin, then immediately rebuy the same amount with fiat. Your stack stays the same size — but Bitcoin circulates as money. Two wallets help.

BIA CRYPTO

Order Matters for Taxes

Spend-then-rebuy = FIFO uses your oldest, cheapest BTC = biggest gain. Rebuy-then-spend those new units = gain near zero. Same spend, different tax.
