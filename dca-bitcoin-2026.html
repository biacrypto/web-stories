What DCA Actually Is

Buy a fixed amount of Bitcoin on a fixed schedule. Every Friday, $100. No timing. No decisions. The schedule decides.

The Honest Catch

Lump sum beats DCA about two-thirds of the time. DCA is not return maximization — it is risk management. It protects you from your own worst timing.

Why 2026 Favours DCA

One 13-year study: DCA historically wins when BTC trades 20-70% below its all-time high. BTC sat near $78K vs a $126K ATH — squarely in that zone.

The Fees Trap

A $1.50 flat fee on a $25 weekly buy eats 6% every time. Use percentage-based fees or free recurring buys — or buy monthly instead of weekly.
