Is a Crypto-to-Crypto
Swap Taxable in the UK?

Short answer: yes. Here is how CGT actually works on swaps — in plain numbers.

BIA CRYPTO

Swaps = Disposals

HMRC treats exchanging one crypto for another as a disposal — even if you never touch pounds. That triggers Capital Gains Tax rules.

BIA CRYPTO

The Gain Formula

Gain = pound value you received minus what you paid for the coins you gave up. Swapping into a stablecoin counts too.

BIA CRYPTO

How HMRC Matches Costs

Same-day rule → 30-day rule → Section 104 pool. The pool averages the cost of all your coins of the same type.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example: £1,000 Gain

Buy 1 ETH for £2,000. Swap it when it is worth £3,000. Gain = £1,000 — below the £3,000 allowance, so no CGT due.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example: £2,000 Taxable

Total gains £5,000 minus £3,000 allowance = £2,000 taxable. At 18% = £360 CGT; 24% if you are a higher-rate taxpayer.

Records You Must Keep

Date, coins, pound value that day, fees, and which wallet or exchange — for every swap, not just sales.
