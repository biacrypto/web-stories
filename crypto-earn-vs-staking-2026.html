Earn vs Staking

The difference every beginner should know before chasing crypto yield.

BIA CRYPTO

Staking = You Secure the Chain

Lock coins on Ethereum, Solana, Cardano. The protocol itself pays you — new coins plus fees. Around 2.5–3% on ETH in 2026.

BIA CRYPTO

"Earn" = The Exchange's Mall

One shiny tab hides five different products: flexible lending, locked deposits, real staking, promo farming, structured trades. Same tab, different machinery.

BIA CRYPTO

Who Holds Your Coins?

On-chain staking: you keep your keys. Earn: the exchange holds everything — your balance is an IOU. Celsius proved what that means in June 2022.

BIA CRYPTO

Who Takes the Cut?

Coinbase keeps 35% of staking rewards. Lido takes 10%. Same coins, same network — on a $50k position that gap costs about $400 a year, compounding.

The 3-Question Test

What asset? How long can it sit untouched? What would a freeze cost you? Rule: Earn only ever holds money you'd keep on that exchange anyway.
