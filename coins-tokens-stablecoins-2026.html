What Is a Coin?

The native currency of its own blockchain. BTC, ETH, SOL — coins pay the network’s fees and secure the chain.

BIA CRYPTO

What Is a Token?

Crypto built ON a blockchain: UNI, LINK, even USDT. Tokens ride the chain — but they can’t pay its fees.

BIA CRYPTO

What Is a Stablecoin?

A token pegged to $1 — USDT, USDC. The parking lot of crypto. But “stable” is a promise, not physics.

BIA CRYPTO

The One-Line Test

Own blockchain? Coin. Lives on one? Token. Promises to stay at $1? Stablecoin. That’s the whole taxonomy.

BIA CRYPTO

The $40 Lesson

Tokens can’t pay gas. Sending USDT on Ethereum costs ETH — a wallet with tokens but zero coin is frozen.

Respect the Risks

Coins crash. Tokens can go to zero. Stablecoins can depeg. Size your positions like you respect that.
