Can You Stake
Crypto in an ISA?

No — no UK ISA holds crypto to stake. Here is what the rules do allow.

BIA CRYPTO

The Short Answer

An ISA shelters an investment you hold inside it. Staking is an activity you do outside it — and no ISA can hold the coins in the first place.

BIA CRYPTO

Why No ISA Can Stake

A wrapper shelters an instrument, not an activity. To stake, the ISA would have to hold the coins. No ISA type — Cash, Stocks and Shares, IFISA, Lifetime or Junior — accepts cryptoassets.

BIA CRYPTO

The cETN Route

A crypto ETN tracks a price, minus fees. It does not stake and pays no yield. From 6 April 2026, new cETN purchases fit an Innovative Finance ISA only.

BIA CRYPTO

The Timeline That Confuses Everyone

Oct 2025: FCA reopens cETNs to retail. Apr 2026: SI 2026/248 moves new cETN purchases to the IFISA only. Old Stocks and Shares ISA holdings are grandfathered.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example

Example only: £1,000 of Ether staked outside an ISA at an assumed 3% earns £30 of rewards — taxed as miscellaneous income, covered by the £1,000 allowance. £1,000 in an IFISA cETN earns no yield; a £100 price gain inside the ISA is tax-free.

BIA CRYPTO

The Tax Outside an ISA

Staking rewards are miscellaneous income at their sterling value on receipt — 20%, 40% or 45%. The £1,000 allowance covers small amounts for 2026/27, rising to £3,000 from April 2027.
