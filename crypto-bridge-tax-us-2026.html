Short Answer: Sometimes

Same asset back on another chain is usually not a sale. A different token out usually is.

The Rule: No Disposal, No Tax

Crypto is property. Tax hits disposals and income — not moving your own coins across chains.

Lock-and-Mint

Coins locked, wrapped version minted. Usually no tax on its own — basis carries over.

Liquidity-Pool Bridge

One token in, a different token out. Usually a disposal — gain or loss on Form 8949.

BIA CRYPTO

Layer-2 Bridge

Same coin, new network. Usually no tax on the move itself — tax comes at sale.

Worked Example

1 ETH, basis $2,000, bridged via a pool for $2,500 USDC: gain $500. Same-asset bridge: no gain at bridging.
