Can You Stake Crypto
in a Roth IRA?

Yes — but only through a self-directed IRA. The 2026 rules, checked.

BIA CRYPTO

Short Answer: Not at a Normal Brokerage

Fidelity, Schwab and Vanguard do not offer it. You need a self-directed Roth IRA with a crypto custodian — and you never hold the keys yourself.

BIA CRYPTO

The 2026 Numbers: $7,500

Contribution limit $7,500 for 2026, $8,600 at 50+. Phase-outs $153k–$168k single, $242k–$252k joint. Source: IRS Notice 2025-67.

BIA CRYPTO

Who Actually Lets You Stake?

iTrustCapital: ETH and SOL. Bitcoin IRA: ADA, ETH, SOL, DOT, SUI. Fidelity Crypto IRA: buy and sell only — no staking at all.

BIA CRYPTO

The Fee Math: 22% of Your Rewards

iTrustCapital takes 22% of staking rewards. A $200-a-year fee schedule on a $7,500 account is 2.7% a year — more than many yields.

BIA CRYPTO

The Catch Nobody Mentions: UBTI

The IRS has never ruled whether staking rewards inside an IRA count as taxable business income. Above $1,000 a year, the IRA itself could owe tax.

BIA CRYPTO

Never Run Your Own Validator

Operating a validator with IRA funds is very likely a prohibited transaction — the whole account can be disqualified. Stake only through the custodian.
