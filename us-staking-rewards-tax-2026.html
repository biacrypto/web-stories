Do You Pay Tax
on Staking
Rewards?

The US rule every staker gets wrong — and the 2026 court case that settled it.

Short Answer: Yes

Staking rewards are ordinary income in the US — taxed in the year you gain control of them, not when you sell.

BIA CRYPTO

The Rule: Dominion
and Control

IRS Revenue Ruling 2023-14: rewards count as income when you can sell, swap, or otherwise dispose of them.

BIA CRYPTO

Locked Rewards
Are Not Taxed Yet

Rewards still locked in a protocol are not income until they unlock. Locked through December, unlocked in January? That is January-year income.

BIA CRYPTO

Worked Example

$1,200 of rewards received = $1,200 of income and $1,200 of cost basis. Sell later for $1,500 and only the $300 difference is a capital gain.

BIA CRYPTO

The Price-Drop Trap

Taxed on $2,000 at receipt, sold for $1,200 later? You still owe income tax on $2,000. The $800 drop is a capital loss with its own limits.

BIA CRYPTO

The 2026 Court Case

Paschall v. Commissioner: a Cardano staker argued tax only on sale — and lost. Rewards credited monthly and sellable at any time were income on receipt.
