Do You Pay Tax
on Crypto
Gifts?

The IRS answer, in plain English — for US holders.

Short Answer: No — At Receipt

A genuine gift is not income for the recipient and not a sale for the giver. The tax is deferred, not erased.

BIA CRYPTO

The 2026 Numbers

$19,000 per recipient a year with no Form 709. $38,000 for a couple splitting a gift. $15 million lifetime exemption.

BIA CRYPTO

Form 709 Is Not a Tax Bill

Above $19,000 to one person, the giver files Form 709. The excess counts against the $15 million lifetime exemption — usually $0 tax due.

BIA CRYPTO

Carryover Basis

The recipient inherits the donor's cost and holding period. Bought for $800, gifted at $3,000, sold at $5,000: gain $4,200.

BIA CRYPTO

The Dual-Basis Trap

Bought $60,000, gifted at $40,000. Sell at $70,000: gain $10,000. Sell at $30,000: loss $10,000. Sell at $50,000: neither.

BIA CRYPTO

Gift vs Inheritance

A gift keeps the donor's old basis. An inheritance steps the basis up to the value at death. That difference can be huge.
