Who Actually Issues RLUSD

The OCC Trust Bank

Approved 26 Oct 2026 to manage RLUSD’s reserves. It cannot take deposits, cannot lend, and has no FDIC insurance.

Moody’s A2 / P-1 — And the Gaps

First investment-grade rating for a stablecoin. But holders do not directly own the reserves, and the issuer is not yet bankruptcy-remote.

RLUSD vs USDC vs USDT

RLUSD wins on paperwork: regulated issuer, trust bank, a credit rating. It loses on size and track record — months, not years.

Can You Still Earn Yield?

Issuer-paid yield is banned under the GENIUS Act. Exchange and DeFi programmes exist — but face case-by-case OCC review and can end.
