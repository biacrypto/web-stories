The One-Line Rule

Taxes do not care about your intentions. They care about events. Every swap, sale, and reward is an event.

Taxable Event #1: Selling

Buy ETH at $2,000, sell at $3,500 — the $1,500 gain is taxed. Cost basis and holding period decide how much.

The Trap: Crypto-to-Crypto

Swapping BTC for ETH with no dollars involved? Still fully taxable. Fifty swaps = fifty taxable events.

Staking Rewards Are Taxed Twice

Taxed as income when received, then again as capital gains if they rise before you sell. Double layers.

What Is NOT Taxed

Buying and holding: not taxed. Moving between your own wallets: not taxed. Paper gains: not taxed.
